What We Make

From the highway to the hockey rink, our work is everywhere you look.

Carrying on a 33-year tradition of sign-making and printing in the Lakes District, now with new equipment, new energy, and community ownership.

Signs & Banners

Storefront signs, site and safety signage, real-estate signs, rink boards, event banners and more. Most of it is printed and built right in our shop on aluminum, coroplast, banner vinyl and metal, with specialty materials sourced from trusted suppliers.

  • Indoor & outdoor signs, any size
  • Grommeted vinyl & mesh banners
  • Road-style signs and custom driveway signs
  • Arena rink boards & sponsor panels

Vehicle, Boat & Fleet Graphics

Your rig is a rolling billboard. We cut and install vinyl lettering and printed decals for work trucks, fleets, vans, boats, sleds and heavy equipment, built to survive northern winters and forestry roads.

  • Door lettering & phone numbers
  • Full-colour printed logos & window decals
  • Boat names & registration numbers
  • Reflective & metallic vinyl options

Laser Engraving, Trophies & Awards

Our laser engraver puts your message on wood, metal, glass and more. From tournament trophy packages to retirement plaques and one-of-a-kind keepsakes, we engrave it all right here in the shop.

  • Trophies, medals & plaques
  • Engraved gifts & memorial pieces
  • Corporate recognition awards
  • Industrial tags & nameplates
  • Full-colour sublimated metal plates and plaques

Who We Are

Cheslatta Owned & Operated.

Corridor:16 Graphics & Signs is proudly owned by the Cheslatta Development Corporation, the economic arm of the Cheslatta Carrier Nation. That makes us a 100% Indigenous-owned business rooted in the Lakes District.

When the shop changed hands, the community stepped up to keep essential printing and sign services in Burns Lake, create local jobs, and build something that lasts. Every order supports that work.

We also produce signage, labels, stickers and other printed materials in Indigenous languages. Ask us about language projects for your community, school or organization.

The drowned trees in our logo rise from the flooded shores of Cheslatta territory. They remind us of where we come from, and why we build things meant to endure.

Our Sister Company

Chief Louie Paddle Company

Rooted in Culture. Handcrafted with Purpose. Made to be Remembered.

Sharing our shop and our laser engraver, Chief Louie Paddle Company revives the Cheslatta tradition of engraved wooden paddles, handcrafted from wood reclaimed on the shores of Ootsa Lake and named in honour of Chief Louie B. Louie.

Custom-engraved paddles, miniatures and wood keepsakes make for unforgettable gifts, retirement presentations and cultural pieces.
